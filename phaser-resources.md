# Free Phaser and Game Asset Resources

## Learn Phaser

- [Phaser Learn](https://www.phaser.io/learn) - official tutorials, the first-game guide, and thousands of examples.
- [Making your first Phaser game](https://docs.phaser.io/phaser/getting-started/making-your-first-phaser-game) - a step-by-step platformer tutorial with its project resources.

## Art and game assets

- [Kenney Assets](https://kenney.nl/assets) - free 2D, UI, audio, and other asset packs. Kenney says assets on its asset pages are CC0, including for commercial use; attribution is optional. See [Kenney's license FAQ](https://kenney.nl/support).
- [itch.io free game assets](https://itch.io/game-assets/tag-free) - a large collection from different creators. Check the license on each asset page.
- [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/) - community-made art with different licenses. Follow the specific asset's license and attribution instructions; see the [FAQ](https://opengameart.org/node/5571).

## Sound effects and music

- [Freesound](https://freesound.org/) - community-uploaded sounds. Licenses vary by sound: CC0 needs no attribution, CC BY requires attribution, and CC BY-NC restricts commercial use. Check the sound's license before using it; see the [Freesound license FAQ](https://freesound.org/help/faq/).

## Suggested starting point

Follow the Phaser first-game tutorial, then use a Kenney pack for placeholder art. Keep a note of each asset’s source and license so you can credit creators when required.
