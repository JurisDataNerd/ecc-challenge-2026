# ECC Future Quest

Shared product language for the ECC Future Quest bootcamp experience.

## Program roles

**Participant**: An enrolled bootcamp learner who completes quizzes and missions.

**Mentor**: An ECC reviewer who assesses participant submissions and provides feedback.

**Admin**: An ECC operator who manages program setup and publishes selection decisions.

## Program structure

**Program track**: A participant's assigned learning path: Professional, Social Impact, or Business. Tracks use the same stage navigation and scene structure, with track-specific quest content.

**Onboarding checkpoint**: The initial Future Base experience a participant enters before Bootcamp L1. It is separate from the three bootcamp stages and does not count as a stage milestone.

**Bootcamp stage**: One of L1, L2, or L3. L1 is the existing Mixel game. The team owns L2 and L3; the final-pitch work belongs in L3. These are the complete bootcamp stages for V1.

**Expedition map**: The participant's out-of-stage menu for viewing and selecting available bootcamp stages. Selecting a stage opens its own scene. The Expedition map is navigation, not one continuous playable world.

**Stage scene**: A separate game map for one bootcamp stage. L1, L2, and L3 do not form connected zones in a shared arena. A participant returns to the Expedition map to switch stages.

## Participant game experience

The Participant game is individual and solo. Public entry offers participant login or a visitor demo. New participants complete the separate Onboarding checkpoint before the Expedition map; returning participants continue their journey. L1 uses the Mixel world; L2 and L3 are separate team-owned scenes.

Movement uses the existing Mixel game's controls: analog input on mobile and keyboard on desktop. Keep the world non-combat and non-multiplayer. Guilds, teammate progress, peer help, side quests, guild reputation, leaderboards, and talent-pool social features are outside V1.

**Quest board**: An in-stage interaction point that opens the stage's quiz and mission list. Reaching a board shows an explicit Interact action; it does not open automatically. Track-specific content is shown for the participant's assigned Program track.

**Personal base**: A participant-specific progression motif that gains one visible milestone for each completed bootcamp stage, growing from foundation toward roof. It reflects L1/L2/L3 completion and does not affect access or selection.

**Future Passport**: The individual progress view. V1 shows only the participant's Program track, XP total, and L1/L2/L3 milestones. Impact, Opportunity, and Readiness categories are deferred.

## XP and stage access

**XP**: A visible, nonspendable total for verified bootcamp activity: 10 XP once for a quiz's first valid submitted attempt, 20 XP once for a mission's first accepted submission, and a fixed 10 XP bonus after final Mentor review when evidence quality is at least 80% of the rubric criterion's maximum. Revisions, retries, movement, and speed earn no additional XP. XP never determines selection or stage access.

**Stage access**: A later stage opens only after ECC publishes the participant's result and the scheduled opening time arrives. Participants who do not advance retain read-only access to previously available work. The Expedition map shows each stage's available or locked state.

## Prototype data

**Demo progress**: Simulated learning activity, XP, submissions, reviews, and access, identified as simulation and kept separate for each individual. Participant authentication establishes identity; it does not make these simulated outcomes verified ECC results. Authoritative progress requires ECC program, review, and selection records.
