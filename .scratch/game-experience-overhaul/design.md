# Interface direction

The memorable element is the existing pixel landscape: use a generous cropped village panorama, with quiet navigation and direct explanations alongside it. Preserve original source assets.

Palette: sky #dcebf0, paper #f5f8f6, ink #163943, forest #446c4e, trail gold #f4c566, muted ink #53696f. Geist handles navigation, headings, and body text; Pixelify Sans is restricted to the brand and short stage labels.

Desktop layout: compact brand/navigation above a split hero with left-aligned copy and a tall landscape on the right; a real three-stage sequence below. Mobile stacks copy before the landscape and preserves both entry actions.

Review against brief: avoid making the hero another gold-framed dashboard. Its illustration communicates the actual world, while the interface explains login, demo access, and the stage journey. No automatic decorative motion, repeated eyebrows, or ornamental counters.
