# Confirm V1 gamification rules with ECC

Status: ready-for-human

Spec: `../spec.md`

## Goal

Record formal ECC sign-off on the accepted planning defaults before treating them as a delivery commitment.

## Decisions for ECC

1. XP: 10 once for a quiz's first valid submitted attempt; 20 once for a mission's first accepted submission.
2. Evidence-quality bonus: one fixed 10 XP after final Mentor review when the criterion is at least 80% of its maximum.
3. Personal base: one visible milestone for each completed bootcamp stage.
4. Stage access: require both the published selection result and scheduled opening time; non-advancers keep read-only access to prior work.
5. V1 navigation: Future Base onboarding, then an Expedition map that opens separate L1/L2/L3 scenes.
6. Individual scope: no Guild or peer features in V1; Passport shows track, XP, and stage milestones only.

## Completion criteria

- ECC records approval or changes to these rules.
- Update the spec and domain context to reflect the formal decision.

## Comments
